08 · Disclosure
How this is calculated
Base pay from the 2026 General Schedule (EO 14368, Schedule 1), plus 33.94% locality pay for Washington–Baltimore–Arlington (Schedule 9). GS pay with locality is capped at Executive Schedule level IV, $197,200.
Why yellow?
Yellow always marks you: your number, your cell, your place.
