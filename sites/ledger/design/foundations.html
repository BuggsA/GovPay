The rules everything is built from.

Every value here lives in tokens.css, the one file the real site and any sibling site will import. Contrast ratios below are computed live from the current theme, so switching to dark mode re-checks them.

01 · Color

Yellow is reserved for "you": your number, your table cell, your state on the map. Green means up and red means down, and both always come with a + or − sign, so color is never the only signal.

Why these fonts: Familjen Grotesk gives the headlines a slightly quirky, printed feel. Hanken Grotesk is very readable and has proper tabular figures, so columns of numbers line up. Spline Sans Mono is used only for the small labels, like boxes on a form. Each one avoids the faces that make sites look generic (Inter, Space Grotesk, Fraunces, Geist) and the government's own Public Sans.

03 · Space

A 4px base. Inside components use 4–16px, between components 24–32px, between sections 48–64px.

04 · Layout
Content · 720px maxThe calculator, the stub, tables and articles. On phones it fills the width with 16px gutters.
Rail · 300px (desktop only)The location picker, related links, and later a 300×250 ad slot if ads are added. Space is held so adding ads won't shift the layout.

One column under 900px. Checked at 390px wide, with no sideways scrolling; wide tables scroll inside their own box with the Grade column pinned.

06 · Motion
$116,071

Motion is for feedback only: a 120ms press, and a 200ms count when a number changes. Nothing bounces, floats or loops. With "reduce motion" switched on, numbers change instantly.
