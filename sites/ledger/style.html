07 House rules
  1. Highlighter means "you." Your number, cell, state and rung get the yellow. Nothing else does.
  2. Numbered boxes label sections, the way a form does.
  3. Rules, not shadows. 1 px lines, corners ≤ 4 px, no gradients.
  4. The stub is the result and also the screenshot card. Its footer carries the URL.
  5. Dotted leaders make breakdowns read like a ledger.
  6. Stamps only for exceptions, like Proposed or Estimate. Not on every number.
08 Never
  • Gradients, glows, glass
  • Emoji or sparkle icons
  • Soft-shadow rounded cards everywhere
  • Hero followed by three identical cards
  • A badge on every number
  • Seals, eagles, .gov navy and red
