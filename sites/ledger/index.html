$ GovPay Ledger

What does your federal paycheck look like?

Pick a pay system and grade. We'll show base pay, locality and allowances for your location and year. This works for civilians, wage-grade trades, executives and service members.

Prototype data. Each figure has a tag. Verified figures match published numbers. Approximate figures are close to the official tables but have not been checked cell by cell. Simulated figures are placeholders, used for states outside UT, MD, DC, PA and VA and for wage-grade schedules. Projected figures are for 2027, which hasn't been published yet. For official numbers, use OPM, DFAS, DTMO and GSA.

Overseas (OCONUS) pay is documented but not modeled yet. See docs/OCONUS.md in the repo.
