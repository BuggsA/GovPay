GovPay: three design directions

All three use the same data engine in shared/govpay.js. Each folder under sites/ deploys on its own.


A · Ledger

A calculator-first dashboard with an official feel. The fastest way to get "my number", plus full pay tables, a compare view and shareable links.
B · Levels

Playful career paths. Level cards, a career growth chart, head-to-head comparisons, and a trip ticket.
C · Atlas

Editorial and place-first. A tile map, a profile for each location, a "same job, every place" ranking, and a plain-language explainer.
